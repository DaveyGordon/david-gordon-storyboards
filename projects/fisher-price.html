What the board is doing

Designing the handoff.

The drawings establish geography, action, visual rhythm and the emotional beat without pretending to be finished film. The point is not polish for its own sake. The point is to give the agency, director and production team a shared picture sturdy enough to build from.

Production logic

Clear enough to use. Alive enough to sell.

A good production board has two simultaneous jobs: remove ambiguity and preserve the spark that made the idea worth producing.
